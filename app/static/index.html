    Day and night watch · NASA, last 48 h

      Photos come from the European Sentinel-2 satellites, which pass every 2–5 days in daylight, so a photo can be up to 15 days old; clouds and thick smoke hide the ground. NASA's VIIRS and MODIS satellites see heat day and night but only fires roughly 50 m and larger. Prototype: it can miss fires and raise false alarms.
